3/11/2022

Universe, an artificial life simulator

- Web based
- uses PhysicsJS, and canvas
- The player sets initial conditions, the simulation runs and you can pan and
zoom live.
- Set multiple simulations in motion and set terminate conditions.
- Set success criteria and repopulate with the more successful ones.
- Music and sound effects.
- Customize animations, background.
- It should be a research tool which is entertaining to watch.
- Record whole simulations and play them back being able to pan, zoom, VCR
controls.
- Play it back with different skins.
- Some simulations will run on the server and you can look in on them.
- share initial conditions with others.

- controls on the left and the viewport on the right.
- viewport resizes to maximum size.
- mouse keyboard and gamepad control

3/12/2022

- A search for soul.  Soul, consciousness, ordered complexity, a strange loop,
is both the most evil and the most good thing that can exist.  Soul is what
makes things valuable.  Soul is to reality what the Higgs boson is to mass.
Without soul there can be no agony nor ecstasy.  There can be no malice nor
nurturing.
- Players/watchers can add a great variety of skin to their simulations but that
skin does not affect the simulation.  There are many states the organisms can be
in and each state can have a different animation.  There can be many, different
organisms which should be just a consequence of their evolved behavior and the
designed appearance and animation can change as a consequence. Skin is also the
sounds that events can trigger.  The background music can change in response to
the whole universe (a particular simulation).  The player/watcher can make
different initial conditions and skins and all this by default is exposed to
other player/watchers.  So the skin is effected by the simulation but not the
other way around.
- I make a system that not only helps people be creative with the simulation and
skin, but also helps nature find soul, and helps people to see that nature is in
the business of growing soul.
- The player can act as a stressor on the system.  Killing all the organisms
is the ultimate failure.  You kill the weak to make the universe stronger.  Woh!
