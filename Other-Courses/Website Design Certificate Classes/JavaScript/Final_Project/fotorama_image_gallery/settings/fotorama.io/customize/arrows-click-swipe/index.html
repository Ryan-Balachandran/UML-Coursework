Arrows, click, swipe

Control the way your users interact with the fotorama with three attributes data-arrows, data-click, data-swipe and data-trackpad:

<div class="fotorama"
     data-arrows="true"
     data-click="true"
     data-swipe="false">
  <img src="1.jpg">
  <img src="2.jpg">
</div>
