Ardour

    Ardour is an open source, collaborative effort of a worldwide team including musicians, programmers, and professional recording engineers. In my opinion, this software rivals ProTools. It is feature rich, runs like it should, and has a very professional look and feel. It also comes complete with a very detailed user manual. Learn more

    Download

    Ardour comes pre-installed with Ubuntu Studio, but the software is updated frequently. You may want to download the latest version. You can try Ardour for free. The demo version is fully functional but it does go silent after ten minutes. In the beginning, I was able to do quite a bit of exploring during the 10 minute window. Once I discovered how much I was enjoying the software, I got on board with the donation.

    There are several VERY affordable donation options. I do recommend creating an account before you make a donation. Depending on your donation, you will be provided with the current version of the software and/or access to future releases. Download Here
