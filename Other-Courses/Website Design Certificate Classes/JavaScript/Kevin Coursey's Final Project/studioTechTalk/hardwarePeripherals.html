What are hardware peripherals?

    Hardware peripherals refer mostly to devices like synthesizers or sound modules. These will have both midi and analog outputs, which means it will likely be connected to both the audio interface (for sound input) and the computer (for midi input) because you'll probably wish to record both.     Buying Hardware Peripherals

    Buying hardware can be expensive, so be sure to do plenty of research before making a purchase. Consider what features you really need. For example, if you're considering buying a synthesizer, but plan to only produce music in a studio, maybe something that fits on your desk works best. Maybe its sequencing abilities aren't as important, because you'll be doing your production work on the computer anyway. If you plan to be playing live shows, you may require something more robust.

    Setup and Installation

    Setting these devices up in the studio is incredibly easy. Simply connect your device's audio outputs to the inputs of your audio interface. If your device has a USB port (it probably will), connect it to the USB 3 card on your computer. Visit the Pc Hardware page for more information.
