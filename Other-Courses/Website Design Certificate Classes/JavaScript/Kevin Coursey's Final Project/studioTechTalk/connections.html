First things first

    Be sure you have the speakers plugged into the amp. Next, plug the monitor outputs from the the audio interface into the inputs on the amplifier. Make sure your audio interface is powered on and connected to your computer. Most interfaces require a USB 3 connection. Verify that you are connected to the proper port. Otherwise, your device may not connect or function properly. Next, turn on the amplifier.

    Let's make some simple connections

    You may want to read up on Jack so you can get a general idea of what it does and why its useful in this studio application. Jack is responsible for all of the client and audio connections that get made between the audio interface, Ardour, and virtual instruments. No need to become an expert, just check out some of the information and when you're ready, open Jack on your computer.

    When jack opens, go to setup, select your interface from the pulldown menu. Click OK and then press the green Start button. Once it starts, click connections. In the connections window, you'll see that the interface's audio and midi ports appear in the various tabs. Connect your keyboard midi controller and you'll see it show up on the midi tab. Now go to the alsa tab and drag and drop the midi controller to the midi through port. That's it! The midi controller is connected to jack and ready to use.

    Make another, more useful connection

    Open Zyn Fusion and wait for it to appear on the midi tab of the jack connections window. Then drag it to system midi capture. The keyboard midi controller is now connected to zyn fusion. Try pressing some keys and check it out.

    Connecting Ardour

    Connect the midi controller you plan to use to control Ardour. Open Ardour, and just like with the midi controller and Zyn Fusion, you'll see the connections for the controller and Ardour appear in the various tabs. Find the master outputs from Ardour and drag them to playbacks 1 and 2. Find the preferences menu in Ardour and select midi controllers. Find your controller in the list and select it. Ardour and the controller are ready to work together now. Try moving some sliders on the controller and watch the corresponding faders move in Ardour. Pretty cool!

    Making More Connections

    Try plugging in some actual instruments or a microphone to the inputs of the audio interface. Make the appropriate connections within Ardour and get recording! For more information on using Ardour, Check out the manual at their website.
